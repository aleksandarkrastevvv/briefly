# Accessibility

## Baseline Requirements

- Keyboard-accessible controls.
- Visible focus states.
- Sufficient color contrast.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Clear labels for selectors and forms.
- No important information conveyed only through color.

## Motion

The animated wordmark must:

- last under 2.5 seconds
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- avoid blocking returning users

## Content

- Avoid jargon in reader-facing text.
- Keep story descriptions under 60 words.
- Use clear labels like “Накратко” and “Ukratko,” not “AI Summary.”
