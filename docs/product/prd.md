# Product Requirements

## Product Name

Briefly

## Primary User

A person who wants to understand the most important public events of the day without reading dozens of articles.

## Primary Jobs

- Start a daily brief.
- Read a ranked sequence of 5 to 8 stories.
- Understand each story quickly.
- Open deeper context when needed.
- Ask source-grounded questions.
- See what a story may mean for their situation.
- Save or share important stories.

## Phase 1 Scope

- responsive shell
- animated wordmark
- market selector
- Home screen
- static sample Daily Brief
- source records
- database foundation
- documentation foundation

## Out Of Scope For Phase 1

- live RSS ingestion
- live OpenAI processing
- user accounts
- payments
- social publishing
- production hosting

## Acceptance Criteria

- The app supports Bulgaria and Serbia through market configuration.
- The Home screen does not show an endless feed.
- Each seed brief has 5 to 8 stories.
- Each story has exactly three key points.
- Sources are visible.
- Unverified source URLs are inactive.
- No API keys are exposed in browser code.
