# Product Vision

Briefly is an AI-native, social-first news and public-information platform.

Its promise:

**Everything important. Briefly.**

Bulgarian positioning:

**Всичко важно за деня. Накратко.**

## Product Belief

People do not need an endless feed. They need a calm, finishable briefing that explains what matters, why it matters and what it may mean for them.

## Initial Markets

- Bulgaria: `BG`, Bulgarian, `bg-BG`, `Europe/Sofia`
- Serbia: `RS`, Serbian Latin, `sr-RS`, `Europe/Belgrade`

## Core Experience

- 5 to 8 important stories per market per day
- concise explanations
- multiple original sources
- visible source attribution
- AI Q&A grounded only in story sources
- personalised “what this means for me”
- social-native outputs that are useful by themselves

## Trust Principles

- Never invent sources, feeds or official claims.
- Preserve original publisher, URL and publication time.
- Clearly mark insufficient source support.
- Do not present generated explanations as legal, tax, medical or financial advice.
