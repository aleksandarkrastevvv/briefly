# QA Checklist

## Consumer App

- Home feels like a briefing, not a feed.
- Market switch changes content and language.
- Daily Brief has 5 to 8 stories.
- Story navigation works with buttons and swipe.
- Completion state appears.
- Save and share controls work.
- Full story shows sources.

## Trust

- Seed or demo content is clearly marked.
- Unverified sources are inactive.
- Official sources are labeled.
- Insufficient source support produces a refusal.
- Advice disclaimers appear where relevant.

## Responsive

- No text overlap on mobile.
- Buttons remain readable.
- Story images keep 16:9 ratio.
- Tabs remain reachable on small screens.
